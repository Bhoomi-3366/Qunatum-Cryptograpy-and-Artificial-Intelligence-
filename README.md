## Before You Present

1. **Update the title slide.** The student names and supervisor were carried over from the original template. Edit them if they differ for this topic.
2. **Verify facts and dates.** The content was written from general knowledge. Please check the following against primary sources:
   - NIST FIPS 203, 204 and 205 (published August 2024)
   - HQC selection as a backup key-exchange algorithm (2025)
   - NIST draft timeline: deprecate RSA/ECC by 2030, disallow by 2035
   - India's National Quantum Mission (approved 2023)
3. **Check the references.** Confirm citation details (venue, year, authors) match your department's required format.

## Suggested Timing

About 1.5 to 2 minutes per slide, so roughly 15 to 20 minutes plus questions.

## Possible Questions to Prepare For

- Why can't PQC alone solve the problem? (It relies on unproven mathematical hardness assumptions.)
- Why can't QKD replace all encryption? (Limited range, low key rates, special hardware, and it does not provide authentication on its own.)
- What is "harvest now, decrypt later," and why does it matter today?
- How can AI itself become a security risk? (Adversarial inputs, data poisoning, model theft.)
